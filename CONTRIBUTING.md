# Contributing

Thanks for taking the time to help.

- **Bugs:** open an issue with steps to reproduce, what you expected, and what happened. Version, OS and logs help.
- **Changes:** for anything beyond a small fix, open an issue first so we can agree on the approach. Keep pull requests focused on one change, add or update tests, and make sure CI passes.
- **Security issues:** do not open a public issue. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

By contributing you agree that your work is released under the repository's license and that you follow the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
